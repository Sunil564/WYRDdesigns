**Writing the name.** WYRD is always all caps. "WYRD Designs" for the studio.
"WYRD Tech Pvt Ltd" only in legal, invoice, and footer contexts.

**Meaning.** Old English *wyrd*, Old Norse *urðr*. Fate. The force that shapes
what becomes. Root of the modern word "weird", which originally meant
fate-controlling, not strange.

**Pronunciation.** Same as "weird".

## 4. Audience

**Primary.** Indian SMB and mid-market founders and marketing heads.
Manufacturing, retail, garments, building materials, solar, hospitality.
Deal size ₹25k to ₹5L. Decision is founder-led, fast, relationship-driven.
They buy proof and clarity, not vocabulary.

**Secondary.** US founders and small brand teams. Buy on portfolio, speed, and
price relative to domestic agencies. Respond well to an unusual name.

**Not the audience.** Enterprise procurement, vendor panels, RFP processes.
Do not write copy aimed at them.

---

## 5. Voice

**Is:** confident, plain, unhurried, specific. Short sentences. Concrete nouns.
Numbers over adjectives.

**Is not:** salesy, apologetic, jargon-heavy, exclamatory.

**Banned phrases**

- craft, bespoke, curated, elevate, unlock, seamless, cutting-edge
- "we are passionate about"
- "in today's fast-paced digital world"
- "your one-stop solution"
- "let's create magic together"

**On the name.** Explain once, in the About section, never in a headline.
Never concede with "but". Wrong: "weird but reliable". Right: "Old English for
fate. Yes, it sounds like weird."

**Formatting rule, absolute.** No long em dashes in any output, ever.
